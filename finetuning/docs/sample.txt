Cloud4Green is a fictional company that runs sustainable data centers.

Its flagship site, GreenNode One, is powered 100% by solar and wind energy and uses liquid cooling to reduce water usage by 40%.

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm UTC, at support@example.com.

The standard hosting plan costs 49 USD per month and includes 4 vCPUs, 16 GB RAM and 200 GB of SSD storage.
