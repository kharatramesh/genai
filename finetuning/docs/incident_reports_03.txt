Operations Incident Review Volume 3
===================================

Post-mortem 1: INC-78742
------------------------

Incident INC-78742 occurred on 11 December 2025 at the Quito data center and lasted 157 minutes. Severity was rated SEV-3. The incident commander was Diego Alvarez, supported by the Data Engineering team.

Root cause: a misconfigured queue consumer caused 62% of requests to fail. 32584 customers were affected. The first alert fired 15 minutes after the change was deployed (change ticket CHG-5927).

Remediation: the change was rolled back and the service recovered fully. Follow-up actions: Rafael Costa will add automated validation by 5 August 2019; Kofi Mensah will update the runbook; estimated cost of the outage was $427,000.

Lessons learned: alert thresholds were set to 14 minutes, which was too slow. We now require a canary phase of 40 minutes before global rollout.
