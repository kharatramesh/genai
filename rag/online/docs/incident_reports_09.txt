Operations Incident Review Volume 9
===================================

Post-mortem 1: INC-28141
------------------------

Incident INC-28141 occurred on 11 April 2020 at the Perth data center and lasted 599 minutes. Severity was rated SEV-2. The incident commander was Rafael Costa, supported by the Quality team.

Root cause: a misconfigured DNS record caused 43% of requests to fail. 47230 customers were affected. The first alert fired 18 minutes after the change was deployed (change ticket CHG-9250).

Remediation: the change was rolled back and the service recovered fully. Follow-up actions: Hannah Cole will add automated validation by 14 May 2019; Marcus Webb will update the runbook; estimated cost of the outage was $386,000.

Lessons learned: alert thresholds were set to 12 minutes, which was too slow. We now require a canary phase of 33 minutes before global rollout.
